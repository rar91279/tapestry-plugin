Reports public fields carrying an annotation that makes Tapestry instrument them, such as @Property, @Parameter, @Persist, @Component, @Environmental or @Inject.

Tapestry replaces access to such a field with generated accessors, which it cannot do for a public field. The page fails to assemble at runtime with Field <name> of class <component> must be instrumented, and may not be public. Make the field private, or protected for a base component.
